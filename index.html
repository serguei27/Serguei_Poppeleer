My story

Trained in engineering, contracts and operations. Shaped by the field.

From site to strategy

Most projects don't go wrong in the ground. They go wrong in the gaps.

My first years took me far from home — cable-stayed bridges in remote country, marine works, shopping centres rising out of desert sand. On site I learned what no classroom teaches: the gap between what was promised, what was written down and what people actually do on a Monday morning.

So I went back to study the gaps — construction law and arbitration, contract and commercial management, then business and operations. Then I took that back to the field — on metros, power plants, pipelines, ports, hotels and retail developments. Today I still get the same quiet satisfaction from one thing: a hard project that finishes calmly.

Delivering through a blockade. A metro programme that kept moving when the borders around it closed overnight.
